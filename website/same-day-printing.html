Same-Day Printing

Needed it yesterday?
Have it by this evening.

National print-and-courier apps quote 2–4 days for delivery. We're a five-minute WhatsApp message and same-day pickup at either of our two Thrissur locations — no shipping, no waiting on a tracking link.

Cut-off for same-day: get your file to us with enough of the day left to print, bind and finish it — the earlier, the safer. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you honestly if today is doable.

  1. Send the file on WhatsApp

    PDF, Word, or photos of handwritten pages — whatever you've got. We'll confirm pages, paper and binding.

  2. Get a quote in minutes

    No app to install, no account to create. You'll know the exact cost before we print a single sheet.

  3. We print while you're still on the way

    Real production on a Konica Bizhub 1100 in-house — not handed off to a distant "print station" we don't control.

  4. Collect the same day — or have it delivered

    Walk in, pay, collect, Mon–Sat. Prefer not to come in? Local delivery is a flat ₹30 and still goes out the same day.

This works best for assignments, project reports, exam material, resumes and anything else where "it'll arrive in a few days" isn't good enough. For large bulk jobs, see our bulk printing page — those may need a short lead time.
