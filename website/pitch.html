The Problem

Printing shouldn't require a treasure hunt.

  • 🗺️ Students waste 30 min hunting for a free shop — walk in, find a queue, walk out. Repeat.
  • 📵 No way to know if a shop is free before making the trip. Every visit is a gamble.
  • 💸 Stores lose jobs to each other based on proximity alone — not quality, speed, or availability.
  • 🧾 No digital record of jobs, payments, or customers. Zero structured repeat-customer pipeline.

Payments

Razorpay Route splits every payment instantly.

When a customer pays, Razorpay Route automatically splits the payment between Printosky's platform fee and the partner store's share — no manual reconciliation, ever.

  • ₹
    Customer pays full amount upfront
    Collected at order time via UPI, card, or net banking.
  • ⚡
    Razorpay Route splits on payment capture
    Platform fee separated automatically. Partner's share transferred directly to their account.
  • ✓
    Settlement in your Razorpay account
    No intermediaries. Direct to the partner's registered bank account.
