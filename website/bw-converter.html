Quick Presets
Parameters
Text pages
DPI 200
Render resolution. 200 suits most book scans; 300 for very small text (slower).
Sensitivity 0.10
Bradley threshold aggressiveness. ↑ saves faint strokes · ↓ cuts paper noise.
Window size (px) 40
Local neighbourhood for adaptive thresholding. Larger = smoother transitions.
Page classification
Tone split 0.30
Mid-tone ratio above which a page is a photo (not text). Text ≈ 22 %, photos ≈ 45 %.
Image / illustration pages
White point clip 220
Pixels above this on photo pages → pure white. Lower if prints still look grey.
