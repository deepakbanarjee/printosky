380 statuses; a novel's re-reading

Manorama Online · 17 May 2019

Dr. Divya, then Guest Lecturer in Malayalam at Ollur Government Arts and Science College, serialised Rajalakshmi's 1960 Kerala Sahitya Akademi Award–winning novel ഒരു വഴിയും കുറെ നിഴലുകളും (Oru Vazhiyum Kure Nizhalukalum) across 380 WhatsApp status updates — 2–3 messages per chapter, 34 chapters in all. Many of her former students took part in circulating it, giving a six-decade-old classic a new readership through their phones.
