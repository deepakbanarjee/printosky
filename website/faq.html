FAQ

Questions people
actually ask us.

Can't find your answer here? Just ask on WhatsApp — we reply fast.

What file formats do you accept?

PDF and Word documents work best. You can also send photos of handwritten pages or JPG/PNG images directly on WhatsApp — we'll convert and confirm the page count before printing.

How does pricing work?

Black & white is ₹3/page standard, dropping to ₹2/page for orders up to 100 sheets and ₹1.50/page beyond that. Colour starts at ₹10/page and drops as order size grows. Full rates, including binding and lamination, are on our pricing page.

Is there a minimum order?

No. A single page is a valid order.

How fast can I get my prints?

Most orders are ready the same day if you send the file with enough of the day left. See our same-day printing page for how the cut-off works.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Both. Pickup from either of our Thrissur counters is free; local delivery is a flat ₹30, and it still goes out same-day. Our book catalogue is a separate service that ships further afield — see the Books page for that.

How do I pay?

We send a secure payment link after quoting your order on WhatsApp, or you can pay by cash or UPI when you collect it in person.

Do you offer student rates?

Yes — black & white pricing automatically steps down for larger orders (₹2/page up to 100 sheets, ₹1.50/page beyond that), applied at billing with no code needed.

What binding and finishing options do you offer?

Staple, spiral, wiro, soft, perfect, project and thesis binding, plus sheet and cover lamination and PVC ID cards. Rates are on our pricing page.

Where are you located and what are your hours?

Two locations across Thrissur: Oxygen Students Paradise in Thriprayar (📞 +91 80896 99436), and Printosky in Nattika, opposite SN College Road (📞 +91 94467 03907). Both open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm.

Can I order without visiting the shop?

Yes — send your file on WhatsApp or use our online order form, get a quote, pay the link, and just walk in to collect the finished job.
