Printosky — Logo System v3

Grounded in a survey of ~130 printing & printer logos. Lessons applied: lead with the wordmark, own the orange (the uncrowded "creative" lane vs the blue sea), avoid the cliché icon tile, and survive 32px / single colour.

Direction A — "The Full Stop" recommended

The orange dot is the brand. Period = delivered. Sun = sky. Ink-dot = print. Wordmark-led like Printo / MOO / Vistaprint; the only colour is the one accent you already own. The icon is a lowercase "p" + the dot — a real monogram, not a stock picture.

Direction B — "Clear Sky"

Keeps the literal sky tie-in: a clean single-colour cloud with the orange dot as the sun (the only colour pop). This is the friendlier, more literal route — handsome, but closer to the generic "icon tile" the survey flags, so it reads slightly less premium / less ownable than Direction A.
