Printosky — Logo System v2

Developing the existing wordmark into a full wordmark + icon lockup. The orange dot (your existing signature) carries into every icon. Pick a concept — A, B, or C.

Three icon concepts

Concept A · recommended

Cloud + sun

Cloud printing. A clean paper-white cloud with the orange dot as a rising sun behind it. Reads instantly as "the cloud," ties to Printo-sky, keeps the orange signature. Scales down to a favicon cleanly.

Concept B

P-cloud monogram

The letter P, as a cloud. Uses the actual initial of Printosky, with the bowl puffed into a cloud and the orange dot anchored as the period. Most "logo-like" as a standalone monogram.

Concept C

Page from cloud

Most literal. A printed sheet (text lines + folded corner) descending from a cloud — "upload to the cloud, collect your prints." Richest meaning, but busiest at tiny sizes.
