Halftone sun — cheap vs premium

Same motif, opposite outcomes. The difference between a $5 stock element and an ownable brand mark is execution: no rays, single spot ink, engineered (not soft) dots, and the sun tied to printing (horizon = a sheet edge) — not "sunshine wellness".

✗ Generic / stock — what to avoid

These are everywhere in iStock/Vecteezy. They read "wellness / cosmetic / honey," not "print shop," and look templated.
Rayed sun
The rays are the cliché tell. Pure stock — reads "spa / solar / juice".
Soft fade
Mushy pastel gradient dots. "Cosmetic/honey" vibe; muddies at small size.
CMYK confetti
"We print in colour!" — the most exhausted print cliché. Rainbow = cheap.

✓ Print-engineered (ours) — same motif, ownable

No rays. One spot ink. Crisp engineered dots. The sun rises over a sheet edge (the horizon line + printed lines = a page), so it reads "print", not "sunshine". Shown coarse → fine.
Coarse
Boldest, most "engineered". Survives the smallest sizes best.
Medium ★
The balance point — clearly halftone, still crisp. My pick.
Fine
Most refined/photographic, but dots vanish below ~32px.
Single-colour
Stamp / embroidery / 1-ink print test — still holds.
