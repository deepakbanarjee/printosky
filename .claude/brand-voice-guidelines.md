## 4. Brand Voice

### Tagline (live on site)
> **"Print it. Perfect. Fast."**

### We Are / We Are Not

| We Are | We Are Not |
|---|---|
| Direct, confident, editorial | Wordy, vague, corporate |
| Student-friendly | Condescending |
| Transparent on pricing | Hiding charges |
| Locally rooted (Thrissur, Kerala) | Generic or placeless |
| Warm but not informal | Sloppy or over-casual |
| Action-oriented | Passive |

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp bot | Friendly, concise, emoji-light | "📄 What paper size do you need?" |
| Rate card / pricing | Precise, scannable | "A4 B&W: ₹3/sheet. Student rate: ₹2 (≤100 sheets)" |
| Promotional posts | Bold, benefit-led | "Bulk prints. Bound right. Ready same day." |
| Order status | Clear, reassuring | "Your job is ready. Come collect anytime." |
| Review request | Warm, low-pressure | "How was your order? A quick rating helps us a lot." |
