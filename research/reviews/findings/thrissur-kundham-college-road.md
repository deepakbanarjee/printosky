# Kundham / Kuntham "The Spear" — College Road, Thrissur

Read over the full reviews pane, ~400 reviews spanning ~10 years, captured
2026-09-04, sort = Most relevant. `corpus/` holds a 53-review sample of this
pane; the counts below were tallied over the full set by hand and are
approximate. Re-run `code_reviews.py` against the full dump to firm them up.

## Shape of the pane

Bimodal, and the two modes almost never overlap. Roughly half the reviews are
one to five words of praise ("Good", "Cheap and best", "Fast service"). The
substantive reviews split into a price/speed camp and a behaviour/wait camp — and
a recurring third group that holds both at once: *"might be cheaper in price but
it gets costlier in regard to the follow up and the mental frustration"*,
*"Printing and photostat is really really cheap so I can overlook other defects."*
That sentence — cheap, therefore tolerated — is the shop's whole position.

## Drivers (why people come)

Price dominates everything: cheapest in Thrissur, bulk slabs, student concession.
Then speed and bulk capacity (*"a thousand page book in less than 30 mins"*),
breadth (spiral/hard binding, lamination, DTP, thesis, plotter, scanning, online
licence applications), remote ordering by WhatsApp/email/pendrive with prepay so
work is ready on arrival, and opening on holidays.

## Questions customers ask that the shop doesn't answer

Rate before travelling (blocked by the no-phone-enquiry policy); phone number;
which branch is the real one; is my job ready; how long; can I send by
WhatsApp/email/pendrive; bulk and student discount; binding types and rates; do
you do scanning / stamp-paper copies; opening hours and holidays; can I pay
online and does it cost extra.

## Owner replies — a cautionary case

Half of all reviews get a reply, which is unusually diligent for the category,
and roughly a third of those replies deflect to "you visited a duplicate shop."
The tactic has been publicly called out by a reviewer who pre-empted it, and
several replies thank a complainant "for your kind words." One recent reply
(a month ago) is a specific, non-deflecting apology — that one is the template.

**Takeaway for our own review handling:** never deflect without checking; never
paste a thanks-for-kind-words template onto a complaint; answer with the specific
remedy. Replies are public and they are read.
