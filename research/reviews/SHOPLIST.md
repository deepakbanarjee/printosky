## Picking who makes the cut

A listing is worth capturing when it has **25+ reviews** — below that the theme
percentages swing wildly on one or two opinions. Prefer shops that are (a) near a
college gate, (b) advertising bulk or student rates, or (c) part of a same-name
cluster. Skip machine dealers and service centres (Excel Associates and similar):
they sell copiers, they don't run the counter, and their reviews are about a
different business.

## Balance the sample

Capture at least a few shops with **high ratings and low complaint volume**, not
only the ones with dramatic reviews. A pattern read only off the shops people
complain about tells you what goes wrong, never what the good ones do
differently — and that second half is the part worth copying.
