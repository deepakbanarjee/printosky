## Why this matters, and what it can't fix

On-page SEO (titles, schema, sitemap, speed) tells Google **what** a page is about.
Backlinks tell Google **whether anyone should care**. For a local print shop, a
handful of genuinely local links outperforms hundreds of generic directory
entries — Google weights local relevance heavily for "near me" intent.

**Set expectations honestly:** a new domain with no links typically takes
**3–6 months** to rank for competitive local terms, even with perfect on-page.
The fastest wins below are GMB and citations, not links.

**Never buy links.** Paid link networks, "1000 backlinks for ₹500" gigs, and
comment-spam services are the single fastest way to earn a manual penalty that
takes months to escape. Everything below is earned or self-published.

## Priority 0 — Google Business Profile (do this before any link work)

For "print shop near me Thrissur", the map pack outranks every organic result.
GBP is not a backlink, but it is the highest-ROI off-page asset you have, and
several tactics below feed it.

- [ ] Claim + fully complete **both** locations (Thriprayar, Nattika) as separate profiles
- [ ] Categories: primary `Print shop`; secondary `Copy shop`, `Bookbinder`, `Digital printer`
- [ ] Website field → `https://printosky.com/` (Thriprayar) and `https://printosky.com/contact.html` (Nattika)
- [ ] Add products with prices pulled from `pricing.html` so the two never disagree
- [ ] 10+ real photos: shopfront, Konica Bizhub, bound thesis, spiral binding, finished PVC cards
- [ ] Post weekly during project season (Oct–Nov, Feb–Mar, Apr–May)
- [ ] **Reviews are the lever**: ask every satisfied student at pickup. Target 5/week.
      Reply to all of them — reply text is indexed and is a free keyword surface.

> NAP consistency rule: the business name, address and phone must be **byte-identical**
> everywhere — GBP, JustDial, Facebook, the site footer, and the `LocalBusiness`
> schema in `index.html` / `about.html` / `contact.html`. Mismatched NAP is the most
> common reason a local business fails to rank despite good on-page work.

## Tier 1 — Citations (week 1–2, free, no outreach needed)

Directory listings. Low individual value, but they establish NAP consistency and
Google cross-references them to confirm the business is real.

| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Priority 0 above |
| Bing Places | Import directly from GBP, 5 minutes |
| JustDial | Highest-traffic Indian local directory; expect sales calls |
| Sulekha | Print/stationery category |
| IndiaMART | Useful for the bulk/B2B side (`bulk-printing.html`) |
| Apple Maps (Business Connect) | Covers iPhone "near me" searches |
| Facebook Page | Must exist even if unused; NAP + link |
| Instagram bio | Link to `printosky.com`, not a linktree |
| WhatsApp Business profile | Set the website field |
| Justdial / AskLaila / Tradeindia | Fill in if time permits |

**Do them in one sitting with a NAP text file open.** Copy-paste; don't retype.

## Tier 2 — Local relevance links (the ones that actually move rankings)

These are worth more than everything in Tier 1 combined, because they are
topically and geographically relevant.

### 2a. Colleges and departments

Printosky already serves college project printing — that relationship is the link.

- Offer department notice boards a **printable PDF rate card** hosted at
  `printosky.com/pricing.html`; many departments link the source page
- Student union / college fest sponsorship → sponsor page link. A ₹2,000 fest
  sponsorship with a website link is better value than any paid directory
- Placement cells and hostel welcome packs: offer a student discount code in
  exchange for a link on the cell's resources page
- **Target list to build:** every college within ~25km of Thriprayar and Nattika.
  Start with the ones already sending project work.

### 2b. Kerala / Thrissur local media and blogs

- Thrissur-focused Facebook groups and local news sites — pitch the
  `online-vs-local-printing.html` piece as a local-business angle, not an ad
- Malayalam education bloggers and YouTubers: `divya.html` / `books.html` are
  genuinely linkable (a real Malayalam phonics book by a named author)
- Local business associations and merchant bodies in Thriprayar/Nattika

### 2c. The author/publishing angle — your strongest unique asset

Dr. Divya M is a published author with a separate site (`drdivyam.com`). This is
a real editorial hook that a print shop normally doesn't have.

- Book listings, Malayalam literature directories, education resource pages
- Press coverage of the author → link to `divya.html` as the order page
- Reciprocal link between `drdivyam.com` and `printosky.com/divya.html`
  (one link each, natural placement — this is legitimate, not a scheme)

## Tier 3 — Content that earns links passively

Two pages on the site are already link-worthy. Most print shops publish nothing.

- **`pricing.html`** — a transparent, public rate card is rare locally. People
  cite it in "how much does binding cost in Thrissur" discussions. Keep it current.
- **`online-vs-local-printing.html`** — genuinely useful comparison content.
  This is the page to pitch to local groups and forums.

**Next pieces worth writing** (each targets a real long-tail query and gives
people something to link to):

1. "University project report format — margins, fonts and binding rules"
   per local university (Calicut, Kerala, MG). High seasonal volume, and
   department pages link format guides.
2. "How much does thesis printing and binding cost in Kerala?" — pricing
   transparency content ranks and attracts citations.
3. "Spiral vs wiro vs hardbound — which binding for which submission"
4. A Malayalam-language version of the core service page. Almost no local
   competitor publishes in Malayalam; the keyword competition is near zero.

## Tier 4 — Ongoing, low effort

- **Answer questions** in Kerala student groups, Quora, and Reddit's Kerala/college
  subs where project printing comes up. Link only where genuinely useful —
  self-promotional dumping gets removed and earns nothing.
- **Supplier and partner pages**: paper suppliers, binding equipment vendors and
  the Konica dealer often maintain customer lists.
- **Testimonials**: writing a genuine testimonial for a supplier usually earns a
  linked mention on their site. High conversion, costs one paragraph.

## Measurement

Track monthly in Search Console + a free backlink checker (Ahrefs Webmaster
Tools is free for a verified domain):

| Metric | Where | Target at 6 months |
|---|---|---|
| Referring domains | Ahrefs Webmaster Tools | 25+ |
| GBP profile views | Google Business Profile | growing month-over-month |
| Impressions, "…Thrissur" queries | Search Console → Performance | growing |
| Map pack position, "print shop Thrissur" | manual check, incognito | top 3 |
| Indexed pages | Search Console → Pages | all 15 sitemap URLs |

**Review cadence:** monthly. If referring domains are flat for two months, the
problem is outreach volume, not the website.

---

## What not to do

- Don't buy links, or use any service promising a link count
- Don't run reciprocal-link exchanges at scale (one natural partner link is fine)
- Don't spam blog comments or forum signatures
- Don't create fake reviews — Kerala local-guide communities report them, and
  GBP suspension would cost more than every ranking gained
- Don't chase national print-industry directories; they carry no local signal
